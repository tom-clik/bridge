When responding to a two-over-one response to a major-suit opening, rebidding the original response suit normally shows six or more cards, and the auction remains game-forcing, but there are exceptions. A new suit is bid when it is the most descriptive call available, which may be 3 cards in ♦ but never in a major. A notrump rebid suggests a reasonably balanced hand, interest in playing in notrump, and a particular strength range.

After opener rebids at the two level, a jump to 3NT shows about 15–17 HCP. A rebid of 2NT shows either a good 12 to a poor 15 HCP or 18+ HCP. With the lower range, responder has a minimum for the two-over-one response and usually shows stoppers in any unbid suits. With 18+ HCP, responder also rebids 2NT, then bids 4NT if opener raises to 3NT, showing the extra strength.

## Repeating a 5 card suit

<bridge type="hand">
KJT94.AK6.963.Q8
</bridge>

There are some hands where opener should repeat a 5 card suit. Over 2♦/2♣ responder should bid 2♠. Responder should expect that this rebid bid of a 5 card suit is possible.
