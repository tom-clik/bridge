**After opener raises responder's major to two, responder's 2NT asks about opener's support and strength.**

Responder has invitational values or better. The main question is whether opener has **three or four trumps**: with only four cards in responder's major, an eight-card fit is not yet certain. 

**2NT is artificial and forcing for one round, not automatically game-forcing.** Opener cannot pass it. A weak responder normally passes the original raise; using the enquiry commits the partnership to at least the three level.

## Opener's replies

Use the same four steps over either major:

| Reply to 2NT | Support for responder's major | Strength |
| ------------ | ----------------------------- | -------- |
| 3♣           | Three cards                   | Minimum  |
| 3♦           | Three cards                   | Maximum  |
| 3♥           | Four cards                    | Minimum  |
| 3♠           | Four cards                    | Maximum  |
{.styled}

Remember **3, 3, 4, 4**, with minimum before maximum. These are artificial replies: after a heart raise, 3♠ shows four **hearts**, not spades.

## Responder's follow-ups

The following is a simple working agreement for these notes:

| Information received        | Practical continuation                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Minimum, three-card support | With an invitation, normally stop in three of the major; consider an agreed minor-suit escape if the 4–3 fit looks poor |
| Maximum, three-card support | With an invitation, normally bid game; consider 3NT with only four trumps and adequate side-suit protection             |
| Minimum, four-card support  | An invitation can stop in three of the major; a game-going hand bids game                                               |
| Maximum, four-card support  | Normally bid four of the major; consider 3NT with suitable balanced hands                                               |
{.styled}

A return to three of the agreed major is a sign-off where available: opener has already described their strength. After a heart raise, the maximum four-card reply of 3♠ passes 3♥, so the partnership is committed to game.

For further exploration, use a new suit as forcing: a stopper-showing bid while investigating notrump after a three-card reply, or a control-bid once a fit and slam interest are established.

**3NT is a natural game suggestion**, not another artificial enquiry. With a known 4–3 fit it will often be preferable to four of the major, but the trump count alone does not establish side-suit stoppers.
