Can be used after opps overcall our NT or we x their weak 2. Bids are essentially the same.

## Points to remember

1. SLOW SHOWS - most common mistake is to jump to 3NT with a stopper. You must relay to show this.
2. After a relay, a bid is invitational if a lower bid could have been made first or a drop if not

## Bids

* 2NT is a relay to 3C
* 3 of their suit direct = 4 of the other major NO STOPPER
* Jump to 3 = 5+GF
* 3x (non jump) = invitational.
* X = "points". There is a modern trend to making this a t/o, but the original way is much easier. A sort of halfway point is to make X of a minor over NT = Stayman
* 2x = drop
* 3NT = no four cards in other major (or both if over minor) AND NO STOP (see notes re X, often preferable).

## After the relay  (slow shows)

* 3x < their suit = drop
* 3x > their suit = invitational
* 3 their suit = 4 in OM and STOP
* 3NT = no major(s) and STOP
