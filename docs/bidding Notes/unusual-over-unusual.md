Over a 2 suited overcall, a cue bid of the higher or known suit is a good raise of partner's suit. Any raise of partners suit is a weak raise.

With a known second suit, a cue bid of the lower suit shows a competitive raise in the fourth suit, and a bid of that suit is weaker.

A double is penalty-oriented: subsequent doubles by either partner are penalties. Do not double with general values, re-open with a x to compete.

Note that many expert players play the lower cue as the good raise. This is prone to being forgotten, keep the good raise the same for one suit known and two suit known bids.
