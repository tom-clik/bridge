1NT - 4♣/4♦ is a transfer to ♥/♠ with slam interest. Opener breaks the transfer to encourage. 4♠/4NT is then RKCB depending on the strain.

1NT - 4♥/4♠ are pre-emptive to play, but prefer a transfer at the 2 level followed by a raise to 4 (shows no slam interest). A splinter after a 2-level transfer shows mild slam interest.
