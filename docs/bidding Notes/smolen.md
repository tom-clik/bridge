**Bid the four-card major to show five in the other major.**

Smolen is used after a 1NT opening when responder has **5–4 in the majors and game-forcing values**. It helps find a 5–3 fit while keeping the notrump opener as declarer.

## Responder's bids

Start with 2♣ Stayman. If opener shows a four-card major, raise to game with ordinary game-going strength.

If opener bids 2♦, denying a four-card major:

| Responder's jump | Meaning                                   |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 3♥               | Four hearts and five spades; game-forcing |
| 3♠               | Four spades and five hearts; game-forcing |
{.styled}

## Opener's continuation

* With three cards in responder's **five-card major**, show support and reach game in that suit.
* With only two cards in that major, bid 3NT.

Neither Smolen bid can be passed. Responder with slam interest can continue exploring after a fit is found.
